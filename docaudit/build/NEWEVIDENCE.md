## [119] CREST-2 primary results (both parallel trials)

CONFIRMED PUBLISHED, and the document's citation is exactly correct. Two parallel observer-blinded RCTs, 155 centres, 5 countries, asymptomatic stenosis >=70%. Stenting trial n=1245; endarterectomy trial n=1240. Primary outcome = any stroke or death to day 44, plus ipsilateral ischaemic stroke thereafter to 4 years. The document's figures (2.8 vs 6.0; 3.7 vs 5.3, not significant) match the published abstract exactly.

## [131] 2024 ESC atrial fibrillation guideline — CHA2DS2-VA replaces CHA2DS2-VASc

NOT cited anywhere in the document, and this is the single clearest content gap. The 2024 ESC/EACTS guideline drops female sex as a scoring criterion, replacing CHA2DS2-VASc with the sex-neutral CHA2DS2-VA score, and organises all management under the AF-CARE pathway (C = comorbidity/risk-factor management, A = avoiding stroke and thromboembolism, R = reducing symptoms by rate and rhythm control, E = evaluation and dynamic reassessment). Anticoagulation thresholds: CHA2DS2-VA = 0, no anticoagulation; CHA2DS2-VA = 1, oral anticoagulation Class IIa; CHA2DS2-VA ≥2, oral anticoagulation Class I, applied identically to men and women. It also recommends against routinely switching DOAC or adding an antiplatelet after a stroke occurring on anticoagulation (Class III). The document uses CHA2DS2-VASc throughout (lines 59, 189, 197, 316, 460, 486) and at line 189 still gives the sex-split thresholds '≥2 (nam) hoặc ≥3 (nữ)', citing ref [18] (the 2021 AHA secondary prevention guideline). Table 3.8 (line 486) is a CHA2DS2-VASc table. For a monograph claiming currency to August 2026 this needs at least a reconciling paragraph.

## [134] ESO guideline on cerebral small vessel disease, part 2: lacunar ischaemic stroke (2024)

NOT cited (the document cites no Wardlaw guideline; ref 130 is the unrelated 2014 Wardlaw HTA on MRI cost-effectiveness). Directly on-topic: lacunar stroke is about a quarter of all ischaemic strokes and is 'typically neurologically mild', i.e. the same clinical population as this thesis. GRADE-based; the writing group found little direct evidence, mostly low quality. Recommendations: patients with suspected acute lacunar ischaemic stroke should receive intravenous alteplase, antiplatelet drugs, and should NOT have blood pressure lowered, following general AIS guidelines; for secondary prevention, long-term SINGLE antiplatelet therapy (not DAPT), blood-pressure control and lipid lowering per current guidelines; smoking cessation, exercise, healthy lifestyle, avoid obesity. No recommendation could be made for progressive lacunar stroke or for other drugs. The single-antiplatelet stance for long-term lacunar secondary prevention is a point a monograph on minor stroke should engage with explicitly.

## [138] ARAMIS — DAPT non-inferior to alteplase in minor NON-DISABLING stroke within 4.5 h (the trial that underpins the document's own recommendation, but is never cited)

Open-label, blinded-endpoint non-inferiority RCT, 38 Chinese hospitals, Oct 2018-Apr 2022; 760 patients with NIHSS ≤5 AND ≤1 point on key single NIHSS items (i.e. non-disabling), randomised within 4.5 h to DAPT (clopidogrel 300 mg then 75 mg + aspirin 100 mg for 12±2 days) vs alteplase 0.9 mg/kg. Median age 64, median NIHSS 2. mRS 0-1 at 90 d: 346/369 (93.8%) DAPT vs 320/350 (91.4%) alteplase; risk difference 2.3% (95% CI -1.5% to 6.2%), lower 1-sided 97.5% CI limit -1.5% vs a -4.5% non-inferiority margin, P for non-inferiority <0.001. sICH 1/371 (0.3%) vs 3/351 (0.9%). NOTE: published 2023, so slightly outside the 2024-2026 frame, but it is the direct randomised basis for "DAPT preferred over lysis in non-disabling deficits" and its absence is a substantive citation gap.

## [141] Pooled RCT evidence: IV thrombolysis in minor stroke (NIHSS ≤5) — the definitive quantitative synthesis

Meta-analysis restricted to RCTs enrolling only minor stroke (NIHSS ≤5). Primary analysis: 4 RCTs, N = 3,364. IVT vs non-thrombolytic standard of care gave NO improvement in excellent outcome (mRS 0-1 at 90 d: OR 0.85, 95% CI 0.70-1.03), SIGNIFICANTLY LOWER odds of functional independence (mRS 0-2: OR 0.71, 95% CI 0.55-0.91), and higher odds of sICH (OR 5.22, 95% CI 1.76-15.48) and 90-day mortality (OR 2.40, 95% CI 1.23-4.67). Subgroup: no benefit in either disabling (OR 0.84, 95% CI 0.38-1.88) or nondisabling (OR 0.82, 95% CI 0.66-1.03) symptoms. PROSPERO CRD42024621714. This is the strongest single citation available for the document's central claim, and it goes further than the document does (net harm signal, not merely "no benefit").

## [143] Extended-window thrombolysis: TRACE-III (positive) vs TIMELESS (neutral) — the document has no extended-window section at all

TRACE-III (China, 516 patients with ICA/M1 occlusion, salvageable tissue on perfusion imaging, NO access to EVT, randomised 4.5-24 h): mRS 0-1 at 90 d 33.0% tenecteplase vs 24.2% standard care; relative rate 1.37 (95% CI 1.04-1.81), P = 0.03. 90-day mortality 13.3% vs 13.1%; sICH within 36 h 3.0% vs 0.8%. TIMELESS (458 patients, 77.3% subsequently underwent thrombectomy, tenecteplase vs PLACEBO 4.5-24 h): adjusted common OR for 90-day mRS 1.13 (95% CI 0.82-1.57), P = 0.45; 90-day mortality 19.7% vs 18.2%; sICH 3.2% vs 2.3%. The contrast (benefit only where thrombectomy is unavailable) is essential context for any 2026-current chapter on reperfusion.

## [144] OPTION — tenecteplase 4.5-24 h in NON-large-vessel-occlusion stroke (the extended-window trial closest to the minor-stroke population)

Randomised open-label blinded-endpoint trial, 48 Chinese centres, Jun 2023-Aug 2025 (final follow-up 28 Oct 2025); 570 randomised, 566 analysed (median age 68 [IQR 59-75], 196 female [34.6%]), non-LVO stroke with salvageable tissue on perfusion imaging, 4.5-24 h from last seen well. Tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg, n=282) vs standard medical treatment (n=284). mRS 0-1 at 90 d: 123/282 (43.6%) vs 97/284 (34.2%); RR 1.28 (95% CI 1.04-1.57), P = 0.02. sICH within 36 h 2.8% vs 0% (risk difference 2.85%, 95% CI 1.16-5.54%, P = 0.004); 90-day mortality 5.0% vs 3.2%, RR 1.57 (95% CI 0.69-3.57), P = 0.28.

## [145] EAST trial — early clopidogrel + aspirin within 6 h after thrombolysis in MINOR stroke (neutral, and the direct counterpart to TAPIS)

Multicentre double-blind randomised trial in China (7 Aug 2022 - 1 Aug 2024) in acute ischaemic stroke with NIHSS 0-5 who received intravenous thrombolysis; randomised to clopidogrel plus aspirin or placebo within 6 h after thrombolysis. 1022 randomised, 995 in the modified ITT population (503 vs 492). Primary endpoint mRS 0-1 at 90 days: 89.7% (451/503) vs 89.6% (441/492); odds ratio 1.00 (95% CI 0.67-1.51), P=0.99 — primary endpoint NOT met. Safety profiles similar. Read together with TAPIS this gives the precise nuance a monograph needs: early DAPT after thrombolysis adds nothing when the deficit is already minor (ceiling effect, 90% excellent outcomes), but helps at NIHSS 4-10.

## [147] Timing of DAPT initiation — large registry analysis directly challenging the document's 24-72 h window claim

Secondary analysis of the CRCS-K-NIH prospective multicentre registry (20 Korean stroke centres, 2011-2023). N=41,530 with minor non-cardioembolic stroke (NIHSS ≤5) or high-risk TIA presenting within 7 days; 25,112 (60.5%) received DAPT. 90-day composite of recurrent stroke, MI and death: 10.7% (DAPT) vs 11.6% (monotherapy), HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.77-0.87). Benefit was concentrated in initiation within 24 h: HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.69-0.79). NO significant benefit at 24-72 h: HR 1.00 (95% CI 0.88-1.15). Possible HARM beyond 72 h: HR 1.25 (95% CI 1.01-1.55). Time-dependent analysis showed the benefit crossing the null at approximately 42 hours. This is observational and therefore cannot overturn INSPIRES, but the document's statement at line 396/403 that the 24-72 h window is supported should be tempered — the observational signal is that benefit wanes well before 72 h.

## [151] Factor XI inhibition after minor stroke/high-risk TIA — OCEANIC-STROKE (asundexian), phase 3, PUBLISHED

Landmark positive phase 3 trial, entirely absent from the document. 12,327 patients (6,162 asundexian 50 mg once daily vs 6,165 placebo) randomised within 72 h of non-cardioembolic ischaemic stroke or high-risk TIA, on top of planned single or dual antiplatelet therapy. Enrichment required a non-lacunar infarct, a history of atherosclerosis, or atherosclerotic plaque on cerebrovascular imaging. Primary efficacy outcome ischaemic stroke: 6.2% asundexian vs 8.4% placebo, cause-specific HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.65–0.84), P<0.001. Composite of cardiovascular death, MI or stroke also lower. Primary safety outcome major bleeding: 1.9% vs 1.7%, cause-specific HR 1.10 (95% CI 0.85–1.44). Adverse events 69.3% vs 70.1%; serious adverse events 19.2% vs 19.5%. Funded by Bayer; NCT05686070. IMPORTANT CAVEAT for accurate use: this is NOT an ESUS trial — cardioembolic sources were excluded and the population was enriched for atherosclerosis, so it does not resolve the ESUS anticoagulation question; no ESUS-specific subgroup publication exists. Accompanying editorial: Boulanger M. Asundexian for noncardioembolic ischemic stroke. N Engl J Med. 2026;394(15):1540-1. doi:10.1056/NEJMe2601859.

## [160] ARCADIA — why the atrial cardiopathy biomarkers may have failed: their predictive performance for incident AF

Provides the mechanistic explanation the document asserts but does not evidence. Of 3,745 patients consenting to screening for atrial cardiopathy, 254 were subsequently diagnosed with AF (96 before randomisation, 158 after). Unadjusted: ln(NT-proBNP) RR per SD 1.99 (95% CI 1.85–2.13); P-wave terminal force in V1 (PTFV1) RR per SD 1.15 (95% CI 1.03–1.28); left atrial diameter index (LADI) RR per SD 1.34 (95% CI 1.20–1.50). In a model with all three biomarkers plus demographics and AF risk factors, only age (RR per 10 years 1.24, 95% CI 1.09–1.41), ln(NT-proBNP) (RR per SD 1.88, 95% CI 1.67–2.11) and LADI (RR per SD 1.25, 95% CI 1.14–1.37) remained associated; c-statistic 0.82 (95% CI 0.79–0.85) with only modest calibration. Conclusion: the ARCADIA biomarkers were only moderately predictive of AF — notably PTFV1 dropped out. Accompanying commentary: Acampa M, Lazzerini PE. Searching for biomarkers of atrial cardiomyopathy at high risk of cardioembolism: what are the missing pieces of the puzzle? Eur Stroke J. 2025;10(2):502-3. doi:10.1177/23969873241276357. ARCADIA thresholds for the thesis: PTFV1 >5000 μV·ms in V1, NT-proBNP >250 pg/mL, or LADI ≥3 cm/m².

## [162] Meta-analysis of the three modern asymptomatic-carotid RCTs (CREST-2 + ECST-2 + SPACE-2)

Directly qualifies the document's CREST-2-only framing. Three RCTs, 3426 patients. Primary composite (stroke or death, or ipsilateral ischaemic stroke): 4.76% (88/1847) with revascularisation vs 6.40% (101/1579) with medical management; RR 0.91 (95% CI 0.47-1.74), P=0.77, with high heterogeneity (I2=74%). ## [170] Timing of high-intensity statin after minor stroke / high-risk TIA (INSPIRES statin arm)

## [172] Stroke-specific effects of intensive BP lowering (ESPRIT prespecified stroke analysis)

Prespecified secondary analysis of ESPRIT on stroke endpoints (11,255 randomised; 3,022 with prior stroke; mean SBP 119.1 vs 134.8 mmHg over 3.4 y). Any stroke: 262 (4.7%) vs 303 (5.4%), HR 0.86 (95% CI 0.73-1.02), p=0.083 — not significant. Ischaemic stroke: 243 (4.3%) vs 261 (4.6%), HR 0.93 (95% CI 0.78-1.11), p=0.423. Haemorrhagic stroke: 23 (0.4%) vs 45 (0.8%), HR 0.51 (95% CI 0.31-0.85), p=0.009 — halved. Landmark analysis: the stroke difference emerged only after 1 year; HR beyond 1 year 0.75 (95% CI 0.60-0.94), p=0.011. No subgroup interactions. The clean message for a TIA/minor-stroke monograph is that targeting <120 mmHg buys haemorrhagic-stroke prevention and a delayed overall benefit, not an immediate reduction in ischaemic recurrence — a nuance the document's flat 'BP control is the single most effective intervention' framing does not carry.

## [173] GLP-1 receptor agonist cardiovascular outcomes — SOUL (oral semaglutide)

SOUL randomised 9,650 patients aged >=50 with type 2 diabetes (HbA1c 6.5-10.0%) and established ASCVD, CKD, or both, to once-daily oral semaglutide (max 14 mg) or placebo. Median follow-up 49.5 months. MACE (cardiovascular death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke): 579/4,825 (12.0%; 3.1/100 person-years) vs 668/4,825 (13.8%; 3.7/100 person-years), HR 0.86 (95% CI 0.77-0.96), p=0.006. Confirmatory secondary kidney outcomes were not significantly different. Serious adverse events 47.9% vs 50.3%. The document's Table 3.10 and section 3.6.2 recommend 'SGLT-2 inhibitor or GLP-1 receptor agonist' at evidence Level I but cite no GLP-1 outcomes trial at all — only the 2021 and 2024 AHA guidelines. SOUL is the current anchor trial and adds an oral route relevant to Vietnamese practice.

## [174] SGLT2 inhibitors and stroke — the document's claim needs correction

The document states at line 534 and in Table 3.10 (line 544) that SGLT-2 inhibitors and GLP-1 agonists both deliver 'reduced major cardiovascular events, reduced cerebral infarction, reduced heart failure and reduced cardiovascular death'. The stroke component of that claim is not supported for SGLT-2 inhibitors. A systematic review of 32 studies of antidiabetic therapy and ischaemic stroke concluded that GLP-1 receptor agonists (semaglutide, dulaglutide) and pioglitazone reduce stroke risk, whereas 'dipeptidyl peptidase 4 inhibitors, sodium-glucose co-transporter 2 inhibitors, and insulin do not seem to affect the incidence of stroke'. The SGLT-2 class benefit is on heart failure, cardiovascular death and renal outcomes, not stroke. Recommend rewording the table so that the stroke-reduction claim attaches to GLP-1 agonists (and pioglitazone, per IRIS) and the heart-failure/renal claim to SGLT-2 inhibitors.

## [175] PASCAL classification — definitive validation in the SCOPE individual-participant-data meta-analysis of all 6 PFO closure RCTs

The document names PASCAL twice but gives no validating data. This analysis supplies them. All 6 randomised trials of transcatheter PFO closure vs antithrombotic therapy, 3,740 patients (1,889 closure, 1,851 medical), mean age 45 (SD 10) years. PASCAL classified PFO relatedness as probable in 1,382 (37.0%), possible in 1,811 (48.4%), unlikely in 547 (14.6%); in the 4 broad-entry trials (n=2,967) probable 860 (29.0%), possible 1,565 (52.7%), unlikely 543 (18.3%). Net benefit over 5 years, absolute stroke reduction vs absolute increase in first-ever post-periprocedural (>45 days) AF: probable −2.5% strokes (95% CI −4.2% to −1.3%) vs +1.3% late AF (95% CI 0.0% to 2.5%); possible −3.4% strokes (95% CI −5.4% to −1.3%) vs +1.1% late AF (95% CI −0.5% to 2.6%); unlikely NO stroke reduction (+0.4%, 95% CI −4.0% to 4.8%) with magnified late AF (+4.6%, 95% CI 0.3% to 8.9%). Conclusion: PASCAL separates the 4 in 5 patients with net benefit from the 1 in 5 with net harm. This is the strongest available justification for making PASCAL, not RoPE alone, the operational selection tool in section 4.2.3/4.2.4.

## [181] GBD 2021 stroke burden, Vietnam-specific

The Vietnam-specific GBD 2021 analysis (the document currently has only a GBD 2019-based source). In 2021 stroke caused 166,954 deaths in Vietnam (males 96,764; females 70,190). Age-standardised stroke incidence 203.36 per 100,000 (95% CI 190.51-271.81), higher than Southeast Asia (187.98) and nearly 1.5x the global rate (141.55). Joinpoint AAPC 1990-2021: incidence -0.34%, deaths -0.49%, DALYs -0.61%; prevalence fell in women (AAPC -0.19%) but rose in men (AAPC +0.26%). Metabolic risks were the largest contributor to stroke mortality (144.89 per 100,000) and DALYs (2,872.37 per 100,000); behavioural risks predominated in males, metabolic in females.

## [183] Early neurological deterioration in Vietnamese minor stroke (NIHSS <=5)

The single most on-topic Vietnamese study for this monograph, and the document cites nothing like it. 839 patients with NIHSS <=5 admitted within 24 h to Bach Mai Stroke Center (Dec 2023-Aug 2024); END (NIHSS rise >=2 within 72 h) occurred in 88 (10.5%), of which 43.2% within the first 24 h, 35.2% at 24-48 h, 21.6% at 48-72 h. END predicted poor outcome (mRS 2-6): discharge OR 22.76 (95% CI 11.22-46.20, p<0.01), 30 days OR 24.38 (95% CI 14.40-41.29, p<0.01), 90 days OR 21.74 (95% CI 12.63-37.43, p<0.01). Predictors of END: admission NIHSS OR 1.24 (1.03-1.49, p=0.02); SBP >150 mmHg OR 1.70 (1.03-2.81, p=0.04); admission glucose OR 1.07 (1.01-1.14, p=0.02); antiplatelet monotherapy OR 3.69 (2.24-6.08, p<0.01); internal capsule infarct OR 2.54 (1.37-4.71, p<0.01); MCA occlusion OR 3.06 (1.29-7.30, p=0.01); ipsilateral extracranial carotid occlusion OR 4.84 (1.26-18.65, p=0.02); haemorrhagic transformation OR 5.72 (1.07-30.45, p=0.04). Note the antiplatelet-monotherapy signal directly supports the document's DAPT argument in a Vietnamese setting. The published protocol is a separate citable item: Nguyen DT, Mai TD, Dao PV, Ha HT, et al. PLoS One. 2024;19(5):e0302822. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0302822.

## [185] Vietnamese stroke unit network - the only quantitative national organisational data

The document states (section 1.2.4) that the stroke unit network 'grew rapidly but remains unevenly distributed' with no numbers; this is the national survey that supplies them. 91 eligible hospitals surveyed Nov-Dec 2020, 66 (73%) responded: approximately 70% had a stroke unit; median 630 acute strokes per hospital per year (IQR 250-1,200); >90% used stroke clinical protocols; daytime nurse:patient ratio 1:4; perceived lack of access to psychologists, occupational therapists and speech pathologists; only 50% had a standardised rehabilitation assessment process. Complements ref 21 (Mai DT 2022), which gives the policy history (MOH Circular 47 of 2016 mandating stroke units; ANGELS implemented in 18 stroke-ready hospitals in 2017) but no network counts.

# Cảnh báo bắt buộc — những điều KHÔNG được viết

1. **Không trích "NOARTERY"** — thử nghiệm này **không tồn tại**. Bằng chứng thật về
   rung nhĩ phát hiện bằng thiết bị là ARTESiA [155] và NOAH-AFNET 6 [156], cùng phân
   tích gộp phân nhóm có tiền sử đột quỵ/TIA [157].
2. **Không gán kết quả cho ENDOLOW, MOSTE, LIBREXIA-STROKE, Lp(a)HORIZON, ORION-4,
   VICTORION-2P, SAFER, CLOSE-2, hoặc thử nghiệm bít tiểu nhĩ trái CATALYST** — tính
   đến 29/8/2026 **chưa thử nghiệm nào trong số này công bố kết quả chính**. Chỉ được
   nêu tên như nghiên cứu đang tiến hành trong mục định hướng nghiên cứu.
3. **Không viết** "8 biến cố chu thủ thuật ở nhánh can thiệp và không có biến cố nào ở
   nhánh nội khoa" cho CREST-2 — con số này **không xác minh được**. Thay bằng dữ liệu
   kiểm chứng được: trong 2.485 người bệnh theo dõi 4 năm chỉ có 104 biến cố nhồi máu
   thuộc kết cục chính, trong đó **70 ca (67%) là đột quỵ nhẹ**; đột quỵ nặng là 4 so
   với 5 (nhánh stent so với nội khoa) và 3 so với 5 (nhánh bóc nội mạc so với nội khoa).
4. **Không viết** rằng ức chế SGLT-2 làm giảm đột quỵ [174].
5. **Không xếp inclisiran ngang hàng với kháng thể đơn dòng kháng PCSK9 về bằng chứng
   kết cục** — inclisiran **chưa có thử nghiệm kết cục tim mạch nào công bố**; chỉ có
   dữ liệu hạ LDL-C [91].
6. **Không nêu Lp(a) như một đích điều trị đã được chứng minh** — chưa có thử nghiệm
   kết cục nào công bố.
7. **Không có hướng dẫn phòng ngừa thứ phát AHA/ASA nào mới hơn bản 2021 [18]**. Bản
   2021 vẫn là văn bản hiện hành; đừng viết "hướng dẫn 2024" hay "2026" cho phòng ngừa
   thứ phát (bản 2024 [41] là **phòng ngừa tiên phát**, bản 2026 [5] là **xử trí giai
   đoạn cấp**).
8. **Phác đồ ticagrelor theo kiểu gen CYP2C19 trong hướng dẫn 2026 [5] là 21 NGÀY**
   (khuyến cáo 4.8.15: ticagrelor phối hợp aspirin 21 ngày rồi ticagrelor đơn trị,
   Class 2b, LOE B-R). Phác đồ **30 ngày** là khuyến cáo 4.8.13 và được gắn với
   **NIHSS ≤ 5, ABCD2 ≥ 6 hoặc hẹp cùng bên ≥ 50%**, *không* gắn với kiểu gen. Bản cũ
   viết sai điểm này.
9. **Tỷ lệ hẹp xơ vữa động mạch nội sọ ở châu Á**: không được viết "30–50%" như một
   hằng số. Tổng quan phạm vi 2025 trên 142 nghiên cứu [186] cho thấy tỷ lệ hiện mắc
   dao động **3% – 89,4% (trung vị 13%)**; CICAS [19] là nghiên cứu **chỉ ở Trung Quốc**
   với tỷ lệ hẹp ≥ 50% trên chụp mạch cộng hưởng từ là **46,6%**. Phải trình bày đúng
   tính không đồng nhất này.
10. **Dữ liệu Việt Nam**: hẹp động mạch cảnh ngoài sọ 50–99% cùng bên chỉ gặp ở
    **62/1.191 (5,2%; KTC 95% 4,0–6,6)** người bệnh TIA hoặc nhồi máu não tại Bệnh viện
    Bạch Mai [182] — thấp hơn nhiều so với con số phương Tây, và là lập luận trực tiếp
    cho việc ưu tiên khảo sát mạch nội sọ ở người Việt.
